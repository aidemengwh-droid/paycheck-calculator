How to Convert Salary to Hourly

To convert your annual salary to an hourly wage, divide your salary by the total work hours in a year:

Hourly Rate = Annual Salary ÷ (Hours Per Week × Weeks Per Year)

For example, if you earn $60,000/year, work 40 hours/week, and work 52 weeks/year:

  • $60,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = $60,000 ÷ 2,080 = $28.85/hour
  • $60,000 ÷ 12 = $5,000/month
  • $60,000 ÷ 26 = $2,307.69/bi-weekly
  • $60,000 ÷ 52 = $1,153.85/week

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert annual salary to hourly wage?

Divide your annual salary by the total number of work hours in a year. For a full-time worker (40 hours/week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours), divide your salary by 2,080. For example, $60,000 ÷ 2,080 = $28.85/hour. If you have 2 weeks unpaid vacation, divide by 2,000 instead.

What is $60,000 a year hourly?

$60,000 per year equals approximately $28.85 per hour assuming 40 hours/week and 52 weeks/year (2,080 total hours). If you work 50 weeks (2,000 hours), it equals $30.00/hour.

How many hours are in a work year?

A standard full-time work year has 2,080 hours (40 hours × 52 weeks). If you have 2 weeks of unpaid vacation, the work year is 2,000 hours (40 hours × 50 weeks). Part-time workers should adjust based on their actual hours.

Does salary include overtime pay?

Generally, salaried exempt employees do not receive overtime pay. However, salaried non-exempt employees may be entitled to overtime. The salary-to-hourly conversion on this page does not include overtime — for overtime calculations, use our Overtime Calculator.
